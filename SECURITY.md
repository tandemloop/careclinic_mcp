# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email dev@careclinic.io with the details. Do not open a public issue that
contains credentials, access tokens, health information, account identifiers, or
data copied from a real CareClinic account.

Please include the affected endpoint or component, what you did, what you
observed, and the smallest reproduction you can give us. We will acknowledge
your report and follow up as we investigate.

## Do not include

- Access tokens, authorization codes, API keys, or session cookies
- Screenshots or exports containing another person's health information
- Account identifiers belonging to anyone else

If you are unsure whether something is safe to send, describe the issue first
and wait for a reply before attaching anything.

## Scope

This applies to the hosted service at `https://mcp.careclinic.io`. The connector
is a personal health tracking tool, not a clinical or emergency service.
